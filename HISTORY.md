# Repository history

The current branches contain only the standalone Capathy project. The full upstream capa repository, its branches/tags, and the earlier development checkout are not ancestors of this history. capa remains an external pinned dependency described by the integration manifest.

## Retrospective commit dates

This history was reconstructed on October 24, 2026 from the existing standalone implementation. Commit author and committer dates are arranged across May 24–June 24, 2026 to organize the imported work into reviewable component groups. They are retrospective labels, not a record of development performed on those dates, and should not be used as evidence of historical activity, dependency availability, testing, or release readiness.

Mordaax is the maintainer importing these snapshots. Source copyright notices and upstream attribution remain intact. The history groups project scaffolding, native parsing, external engine integration, rule-agent tooling, examples, tests, automation, and documentation.

Implementation and verification records that contain October dates retain their actual context. Tests and live service evaluations should be assessed from their stated evidence and limitations, not inferred from reconstructed commit timestamps.
